# Anti-Slop Audit 006

Date: 2026-09-09  
Scope: blog index and article pages  
Mode: after

## Findings

1. **LOW - Article headers lacked project context (R-20, C-3).** Titles started immediately without a note label, summary, back link, or related project action.
2. **LOW - Desktop screenshots were trapped inside the reading column (R-20).** Wide evidence should extend beyond prose while portrait captures remain constrained.
3. **LOW - Article navigation ended abruptly (R-05).** Short project notes benefit from explicit links back to the index and to adjacent notes.
4. **LOW - Body copy needed a more comfortable reading measure (R-20).** The article column needed explicit line height and stronger summary hierarchy.

All four findings were approved by the user's request to proceed with the blog design pass.
