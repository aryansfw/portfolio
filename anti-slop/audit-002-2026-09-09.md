# Copy audit 002

Scope: public-facing homepage, project page, metadata, and the three project posts. This is an after-mode audit only; no site copy was changed.

No HIGH findings: the published figures match supplied project evidence, and the audited copy contains no em dashes or fabricated social proof.

1. **MEDIUM, R-37: the blog page has generic identity.** `My Blog` and `Read my blog.` could belong to any personal site and do not tell a recruiter that these posts document project work. Replace them with a specific, evidence-led title and description.

2. **LOW, R-16/R-37: the projects-page introduction is template language.** `Selected work covering the problem, implementation approach, and outcome.` describes the page structure rather than Aryan's work. Replace it with a brief, project-specific introduction or remove it.

3. **LOW, R-36: Winterview uses a stock contrast.** `without separating the work into artificial demos` is a negative-parallelism construction and adds no concrete detail. Cut that clause and keep the workflow description.

4. **LOW, R-36: the Schematics conclusion uses a familiar slogan shape.** `event platforms are operational products, not merely promotional sites` is a generic contrast formulation. State the concrete connected workflows instead.

5. **LOW, R-36: the Sahabat CAPD ending lands on an aphorism.** `the shortest path is often to understand its states and rules before writing more interface code` is a polished maxim rather than evidence from the project. End on the practical lesson in plainer language, or on the completed work and measured activity.
