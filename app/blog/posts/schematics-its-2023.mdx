Schematics ITS 2023 needed to serve two very different audiences: people discovering the event and participants moving through registration and verification. My work focused on making those paths clear, responsive, and connected to the event platform's backend APIs.

The result was an event website that served more than 3,000 participants.

## From event information to action

Event sites often begin as landing pages, but the useful work starts when visitors need to take action. I contributed responsive public pages alongside bootcamp and seminar registration flows, payment-proof uploads, and participant-facing status updates.

The goal was to keep the path from learning about an event to completing registration understandable on both desktop and mobile screens.

## Supporting the operations team

The platform also needed administration workflows for reviewing participant information and registration status. I built interface work for those review screens so staff could move from participant data to the next required action without relying on disconnected tools.

I also reviewed frontend work from three staff members, helping maintain consistency in implementation and interface quality as the event platform grew.

## What the project reinforced

Schematics reinforced that event platforms are operational products, not just promotional sites. Public pages, registration, document submission, and review work need to form one coherent journey. Responsive behavior matters especially because participants may start and finish the process on different devices.

## Result

I contributed frontend features and API-integrated workflows for an event platform serving 3,000+ participants. The public site remains available at [schematics-23.vercel.app](https://schematics-23.vercel.app/).
